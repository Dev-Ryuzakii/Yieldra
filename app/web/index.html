Your sponsorships

    What happens to the money

    Who checks the photo?
    An AI model compares the farmer's photo with what that stage should look like and says how sure it is. Clear passes go through. Unsure ones wait for a person. A photo that fails, or has been used before, releases nothing.
    How does the farmer get paid?
    In naira, to their own bank account. The account name is verified by the bank before any payment, and every payout is confirmed by the bank rather than taken on trust.
    What if I change my mind?
    Stop from your sponsorship page at any time. What has been paid stays with the farmer, and nothing more is collected. With PayPal, your saved account is deleted.
    Is this an investment?
    No. You are paying for a farm to be planted and harvested. You get photos and updates, not a financial return.

    Dollar payments run on PayPal. Naira payments run on Tuago.
